## English

**Fixes audio that repeated now and then on Linux.** This is a release of **Digimon World 4 — Recomp**, a native port of Digimon World 4 (original Xbox, 2005) made by static recompilation. No emulator. Still early and experimental.

> ⚠️ **No game files included.** You need your own, legally obtained Digimon World 4 disc for the Xbox, **NTSC-U** (title ID `42410002`). Never share game files.

> **Only the beginning of the game has been tested:** Terminal Area (the lobby), Central Area, Death Valley and Humid Cave.

### What changed

**Linux**

- **Music and voices no longer repeat a fraction of a second every so often.** The game's audio threads were missing display refreshes they were waiting for, the streamed audio ran dry and its last third of a second played again. It happened mostly when the computer had power to spare, and it was there in every earlier version. Nothing to configure.

**Windows**

- No change in behaviour: the problem did not exist there. The package is the same as v0.5.0 with the new version number.

### Updating

- **From v0.5.0:** open the launcher; it offers v0.5.1 on the **Play** screen (**Update now**). This is the first update delivered that way, so if anything goes wrong, download the package below as usual and please report it. The Linux `.tar.gz` only opens this page.
- **From v0.4.1 or earlier:** download the package below; those versions cannot update themselves.

Saves and settings are kept (`~/.local/share/dw4-recomp/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\dw4-recomp\` on Windows).

### Getting started

- **Windows:** download `DigimonWorld4-Recomp-v0.5.1-windows-x86_64.zip`, extract all of it, open `DigimonWorld4-Recomp.exe` (SmartScreen: **More info → Run anyway**), choose the image (`.iso`) of your disc and click **PLAY**. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a GPU with a Vulkan or OpenGL 3.3 driver.
- **Linux:** download `DigimonWorld4-Recomp-v0.5.1-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable (`chmod +x`), open it, choose the image (`.iso`) of your disc and click **PLAY**. x86-64, glibc 2.38 or newer, a GPU with a Vulkan or OpenGL 3.3 driver. Without FUSE: `--appimage-extract-and-run`, or the `.tar.gz`.

### Tested

On one machine only (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070, AMD Ryzen 7 5700X).

- **Played on Linux with the AppImage of this release:** the repeating audio is gone.
- **Measured, no window:** before the fix 4 of 5 runs from boot to the lobby had repeated audio; after it, 0 of 5, and the audio threads no longer miss a refresh. The AppImage itself was measured the same way.
- Boot to the lobby matches the reference frames on Vulkan and OpenGL, on Linux and on the Windows build under Wine.

### Known issues

- **The update from v0.5.0 has not been exercised by anyone yet**; see "Updating" above.
- Everything listed for [v0.5.0](https://github.com/lorransouzaaguiar/Digimon-World-4-Recomp/releases/tag/v0.5.0) still applies: more than one controller untested, nothing tested on real Windows, the floor that darkens for a moment, and the possible quit when skipping the intro movie with START (seen in 3 of about 55 automated runs).

### Reporting a problem

In the launcher: **Support → Create report**, and attach the file to an issue at https://github.com/lorransouzaaguiar/Digimon-World-4-Recomp/issues. The report has no game files and not your home folder's name.

### Checksums

`SHA256SUMS-v0.5.1.txt` lists the SHA-256 of the three downloads.

> ⚠️ **Os arquivos do jogo não vêm junto.** Você precisa do seu próprio disco de Digimon World 4 para Xbox, obtido legalmente, **NTSC-U** (title ID `42410002`). Nunca compartilhe arquivos do jogo.

> **Só o começo do jogo foi testado:** Terminal Area (o lobby), Central Area, Death Valley e Humid Cave.

**Linux**

- **Música e vozes não repetem mais uma fração de segundo de tempos em tempos.** As threads de som do jogo perdiam atualizações de tela pelas quais esperavam, o som em fluxo ficava sem dados e o último terço de segundo tocava de novo. Acontecia mais quando o computador estava folgado, e existia em todas as versões anteriores. Não há nada a configurar.

**Windows**

- Nenhuma mudança de comportamento: lá o problema não existia. O pacote é o da v0.5.0 com o número de versão novo.

- **Vindo da v0.5.0:** abra o launcher; ele oferece a v0.5.1 na tela **Jogar** (**Atualizar agora**). Esta é a primeira atualização entregue desse jeito: se algo der errado, baixe o pacote abaixo como sempre e, por favor, relate. O `.tar.gz` de Linux só abre esta página.
- **Vindo da v0.4.1 ou de uma anterior:** baixe o pacote abaixo; essas versões não se atualizam sozinhas.

Saves e opções são mantidos (`~/.local/share/dw4-recomp/` no Linux, `%APPDATA%\dw4-recomp\` no Windows).

### Como começar

Windows 10 ou 11, 64 bits, placa de vídeo com driver Vulkan ou OpenGL 3.3.

Sem FUSE: `--appimage-extract-and-run`, ou o `.tar.gz`.

Em uma máquina só (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070, AMD Ryzen 7 5700X).
